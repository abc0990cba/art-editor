# Tracer sample image credits

The six sample images shipped with the built-in `vt*` trace presets
(`src/engine/trace/params.ts`) are the demo images of the
[VTracer web app](https://www.visioncortex.org/vtracer/), kept here so the presets
behave offline exactly like the site. All rights belong to their authors; they are
redistributed here solely as demo material, mirroring the credits the VTracer site shows:
